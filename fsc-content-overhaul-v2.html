Step 04
Five non-negotiables. The rules that make it stick.
1
Speak from experience, not authority
"Here's what I've noticed" and "In 10 years of coaching" are more powerful than any certification. Your lived experience IS the credential. Lead with it every time.
2
Hook in the first 2 seconds or 2 words
Every reel, every caption. Write the hook last, after you know the point. If it doesn't stop the scroll in the first line, it doesn't matter what comes after.
3
One post, one idea
You don't have to say everything every time. Pick one thing and say it well. The urge to over-explain is what makes posts feel like homework — for you and the reader.
4
Voice note to caption — always
You think better out loud. Voice note your idea, drop the transcript into the generator below, and let Claude clean it up in your voice. Your raw voice is the asset.
5
Done beats perfect every time
Your most viral content will be the one you almost didn't post. The 52nd birthday TikTok wasn't perfectly planned. Consistency compounds. Post it anyway.
