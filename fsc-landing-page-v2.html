Meet Christin

I coach what I live.
All of it.

I'm a certified personal trainer who has spent over a decade working exclusively with women 35+. I built Feminine Strength Co. because I got tired of watching women — and honestly, myself — get stuck in a spiral of over-researching, over-identifying with a label, and feeling worse for it.

At some point I stopped looking for the why and started just moving. Lifting gave me back a sense of myself that no amount of research ever did. That's what I bring to every client.

I'm 52. I still train hard. I'm a solo parent. I'm in the thick of the same season you're navigating. I don't coach from a distance — I coach from inside it.
